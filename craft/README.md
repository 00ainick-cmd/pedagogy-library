# Lesson Craft

How a lesson page is made, pattern by pattern. The principles in `../principles/` say why a move teaches; these notes say
how to build it, when it works, when it does not, and where a real lesson already does it well.

| Section | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [interactions/](interactions/README.md) | 31 interaction patterns mapped to Rise 360, Storyline, Evolve and H5P blocks: hotspots on real documents, one-card sorts, flip cards, steppers, timelines, scenarios, picture-option questions, find the fault, build the circuit and more. Also [the mixing guide](interactions/mixing-guide.md) (keep every lesson fresh) and [the gaps](interactions/gaps.md) still to build. |
| [media-motion/](media-motion/README.md) | The graphics standard and "fix the graphic" checklist, when animation teaches, the simulation recipe and "make the sim better" checklist, 3D, HyperFrames video and scroll techniques inside a paged lesson, and vetted open libraries. |
| [history-incidents/](history-incidents/README.md) | How to write a history page and an incident page from the official report, how to verify an image licence, and a map of people and real accidents for every CAET lesson. |
| [visual-design/](visual-design/README.md) | The Electric Ink look written down: tokens, type, the accent per room, headers, heroes, cards, captions and credits, phone rules, and the parts bin of symbols. |

Each pattern note links to the chapters that justify it and names a lesson page you can open to see it working.
