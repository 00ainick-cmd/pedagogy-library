03 Concept Page · teach

The click is the reading

Click torque

You stop when the wrench clicks. That click is the torque value you set. Turning past it is not more tightness. It is a broken spec.

Set

Dial the wrench to the spec before you put it on the fastener.

Pull

Smooth pull until the click. One click. Then stop.

Never

Do not use a click wrench as a breaker bar. That wrecks the calibration.
