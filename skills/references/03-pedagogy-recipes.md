# Pedagogy Recipes by Content Shape

These recipes give a recommended card sequence for each content shape in the tag vocabulary.
Pick the shape that best describes what the lesson is teaching, then follow the recipe.

Choose individual cards using [00-job-to-card-chooser.md](00-job-to-card-chooser.md).
Verify swap hygiene with [01-swap-protocol-checklist.md](01-swap-protocol-checklist.md).
Write the knowledge check to the standard in [02-item-writing.md](02-item-writing.md).

Card names and numbers refer to the full definitions in `references/slide-types-catalog.md`.

---

## Conceptual

A lesson grounded in a principle, definition, or framework, with no procedure attached.
The goal is that the learner can explain the concept and distinguish it from near neighbors.

**Recipe:** Cold Open (01) -> Concept Page (03) -> Reveal Board (06) -> Predict, then Reveal (11) -> knowledge check

- 01 sets the tension (why this concept matters in the field).
- 03 teaches the term, definition, and key distinctions.
- 06 forces active retrieval of those distinctions.
- 11 commits the learner to a prediction before showing confirming data.
- Check assesses at application level, not definition recall.

---

## Procedural

A lesson where the sequence of steps carries a real consequence: skip a step or reverse two,
and the outcome changes.

**Recipe:** Cold Open (01) -> Concept Page (03) -> Explorable Schematic (07) -> Sequence It (09) -> knowledge check

- 01 opens with the consequence of getting the order wrong.
- 03 explains the principle behind the sequence (why the order matters).
- 07 lets the learner explore the system the procedure acts on, before performing it.
- 09 tests the correct order under a graded gate.
- Check assesses a branch or exception in the procedure, not a step-name recall.

---

## Data

A lesson whose core claim is carried by a number or a measured relationship: prevalence,
failure rates, before-and-after comparisons.

**Recipe:** Cold Open (01) -> The Number (04) -> Predict, then Reveal (11) -> The Evidence (05) -> knowledge check

- 01 establishes why the data matters.
- 04 lands the headline figure, big.
- 11 forces the learner to predict the trend or comparison before seeing the chart.
- 05 proves the full relationship with a sourced chart and a takeaway annotation.
- Check asks the learner to interpret or apply the data, not retrieve the figure.

---

## Comparative

A lesson whose goal is discrimination: the learner must tell two things apart under
realistic conditions, not just name them.

**Recipe:** Cold Open (01) -> Concept Page (03) -> Compare Wipe (10) -> Reveal Board (06) -> knowledge check

- 01 opens on a consequence of misidentification.
- 03 states the criteria that separate the two things.
- 10 gives the learner a draggable comparison so they can practice reading the difference.
- 06 tests retrieval of the distinguishing tells.
- Check presents a scenario and requires a GO/NO-GO or classify decision.

---

## Diagnostic

A lesson that teaches fault-finding: reading a symptom, narrowing a cause, or making a
GO/NO-GO call with incomplete information.

**Recipe:** Cold Open (01) -> The Evidence (05) -> Live Instrument (08) -> Compare Wipe (10) -> Sequence It (09) -> knowledge check

- 01 opens on a real-world fault scenario or accident.
- 05 shows the data pattern that signals the fault (the evidence you would see).
- 08 lets the learner manipulate the system input and see where the threshold sits.
- 10 trains the eye to discriminate the defective indication from the acceptable one.
- 09 sequences the isolation steps in the correct diagnostic order.
- Check presents a symptom set and requires the learner to name the next action.

---

## Regulatory

A lesson grounded in a rule, standard, or requirement that the learner must know and apply
correctly on the job.

**Recipe:** Cold Open (01) -> Concept Page (03) -> The Number (04) -> Predict, then Reveal (11) -> knowledge check

- 01 opens with a consequence of non-compliance (the reason the rule exists).
- 03 states the regulation, its scope, and any key distinctions (e.g., applies to Part 91
  but not Part 135).
- 04 lands a figure from the regulatory record that makes the rule concrete (a fine, a rate,
  a time limit).
- 11 asks the learner to predict the compliance outcome in a scenario before revealing the
  correct ruling.
- Check presents a scenario requiring the learner to apply, not recite, the rule.

---

## System

A lesson on how interconnected parts work together as a whole: signal flow, power
distribution, avionics bus architecture, pneumatic routing.

**Recipe:** Cold Open (01) -> Concept Page (03) -> Explorable Schematic (07) -> Live Instrument (08) -> knowledge check

- 01 opens with a system failure or a capability the system enables.
- 03 names the subsystems and states the one-sentence job of the whole.
- 07 lets the learner explore each subsystem by tapping it; they build the mental map.
- 08 lets the learner manipulate one input and see the system-level consequence.
- Check asks the learner to trace what happens to a signal or fault through the system.

---

## Historical

A lesson grounded in an event, accident, or trend that makes the stakes concrete and gives
the rest of the lesson a "why it matters" anchor.

**Recipe:** Cold Open (01) -> The Number (04) -> The Evidence (05) -> Concept Page (03) -> knowledge check

- 01 names the event and creates tension (what happened, and who it happened to).
- 04 lands the key figure from the event (a death toll, a delay count, a dollar amount,
  a time interval).
- 05 shows the trend or pattern the event sits inside (not an isolated incident).
- 03 draws the principle or lesson the event demonstrates, now that the stakes are established.
- Check asks the learner to apply the principle from the event to a fresh scenario.
